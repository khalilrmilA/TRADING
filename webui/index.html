📖 How the AI trades

Everything here is simulated with $100 of play money. Nothing is real, ever.

Step 1 — It reads the charts with 4 classic methods

  • Trend following — buys when the fast average (EMA 12) rises above the slow one (EMA 26) and price is above its 50-bar average → the market is trending up. Sells on the opposite.
  • Mean reversion — buys when price falls below the lower Bollinger Band while RSI < 30 (oversold: "stretched too far down, likely to bounce"). Takes profit when price returns to the middle band.
  • Breakout — buys when price breaks above the highest point of the last 20 bars with strong volume (1.5× average) → new momentum starting.
  • RSI + MACD confirmation — buys when MACD crosses up and RSI > 50, so two independent momentum signals agree.

Step 2 — The strategies vote

Each method votes up, down or neutral on every coin. A coin is only considered when at least 2 of the 4 agree on the same direction. The strongest setups are ranked first.

Step 3 — The local AI confirms

The best candidates are shown to qwen3:14b (running on your GPU) with the full chart picture: trend direction, distance to recent highs/lows (support & resistance), RSI, MACD, volatility and volume. The AI answers bullish / bearish / neutral with a confidence score and written reasoning. The bot only buys when the AI agrees with the vote at ≥ 60% confidence. You can read the AI's reasoning on every trade in the feed.

Step 4 — Position size is based on risk, not gut feeling

Size is computed from the coin's volatility (ATR) so that if the stop-loss is hit, you lose about $1 (1%) of the account: size = (equity × 1%) ÷ (2 × ATR), and never more than 35% of the account in one coin.

Step 5 — Every trade has an exit plan before it starts

  • Stop-loss at entry − 2×ATR — cuts the loss automatically if the chart turns.
  • Take-profit at entry + 3×ATR — locks the win at 1.5× the risk.
  • The bot also exits early if the strategy votes flip against the trade.

Safety rules (always on)

  • Max 1% risk per trade · max 5 open trades · max 35% of the account per coin
  • Trading pauses for the day after −3% daily loss
  • Circuit breaker: everything halts if the account drops 10% from its peak

Reading the candles yourself

Each candle = one hour. Green body: price closed higher than it opened. Red: it closed lower. The thin wicks show the highest and lowest price of that hour. The blue line on the equity chart is your $100 — up and to the right is the goal 😉.
